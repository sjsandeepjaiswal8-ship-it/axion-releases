Choose your preferred way to install:

METHOD 1: Instant 1-Click Batch (Recommended)
---------------------------------------------
1. Double-click "Install-AXION.bat" in this folder.
2. It automatically unblocks the files and launches the setup wizard.

METHOD 2: Terminal 1-Liner (Fastest)
------------------------------------
Open Windows Terminal or PowerShell and run:
powershell -c "irm https://sjsandeepjaiswal8-ship-it.github.io/axion-studio/install.ps1 | iex"

METHOD 3: Standalone Setup
--------------------------
Double-click "AXION-Setup-Pro.exe" to begin the installation.
